# Cosmoid Concept Catalog

This document is the working reference for Cosmoid creature concepts currently under consideration. Details are exploratory unless they have already been implemented in the game; names, typings, lore, and evolution paths may change.

Last consolidated: September 21, 2026

## Developed Concepts

### Dormatee — The Shared-Dream Cosmoid

- **Typing:** Psychic
- **Name concept:** _Dormire_ ("to sleep") or _dormant_ + _manatee_, giving the name a simple, warmly sleepy sound.
- **Core concept:** A rare Psychic manatee whose naps calm an entire lagoon by linking nearby sleeping minds through its dense tactile whiskers.
- **Visual identity:** A round blue-lavender manatee curled into a floating resting pose, with a broad paddle tail, dense pearl-tipped whisker fan, and exactly one pearly dream orb.
- **Story placement:** Proposed rare wildlife encounter for Level 7's no-objective, cannot-lose island stop. The player may catch it or leave it sleeping in a sheltered lagoon.
- **Status:** Final name and standalone identity approved September 18, 2026. Icon, directional battle assets, Psychic element support, species registration, gameplay defaults, and manifest are implemented; custom dream techniques and Level 7 runtime integration remain pending.
- **Dedicated record and concept art:** [`docs/cosmoids/dormatee.md`](./docs/cosmoids/dormatee.md)

### Yokodillo — The Sumo Armadillo Cosmoid

- **Typing:** Fighting
- **Name concept:** _Yokozuna_ + _armadillo_, joining sumo's highest rank to the creature's animal basis.
- **Core concept:** A jovial armadillo brawler that plants itself in a sumo stance, welcomes an opponent's strongest shove, then curls into a rolling countercharge when finally moved.
- **Visual identity:** A huge cream-bellied round torso inside layered brown armadillo armor, broad raised palms, a cheerful grin, and two stout feet firmly planted in a low, wide stance.
- **Story placement:** Proposed Level 7 wildlife for the relaxed no-objective, cannot-lose island stop, where it turns stretching and friendly sparring into playful pushing contests.
- **Status:** Production-integrated with a game icon, directional battle art, Fighting element support, gameplay data, a dated manifest, and approved journal artwork showing its unexpectedly gentle bark-lifting foraging behavior. Custom sumo techniques and Level 7 runtime placement remain open.
- **Dedicated record and concept art:** [`docs/cosmoids/yokodillo.md`](./docs/cosmoids/yokodillo.md)

### Poguin — The Hopping Penguin Cosmoid

- **Typing:** Electric/Water
- **Name concept:** Pogo + penguin, pronounced "POH-gwin".
- **Core concept:** An exuberant rockhopper-inspired penguin whose oversized webbed feet turn rhythmic hops and stamps into charged splashes and coordinated movement.
- **Visual identity:** Selected penguin concept 9, with its existing appearance preserved: slender charcoal-and-cream body, sweeping golden eyebrow plumes, and enormous green webbed feet.
- **Story placement:** Selected supporting enemy direction for Level 6's Ironbell officer confrontation. Jumping is part of the intended direction; hop-hop-stomp timing, hauling effects, and Voltusk interactions remain proposals.
- **Status:** Final name selected and concept documented September 7, 2026. Production assets and gameplay integration remain pending.
- **Dedicated record and concept art:** [`docs/cosmoids/poguin.md`](./docs/cosmoids/poguin.md)

### Palmit — The Castaway Cosmoid

- **Core concept:** A freshwater crab that uses a coconut as a boat to protect itself from salt water while traveling across the ocean.
- **Typing:** Grass
- **Entry:** A freshwater crab that uses a coconut as a boat to protect itself from salt water when traveling the ocean.
- **Evolution ideas:** It leaves the coconut after growing a stronger natural shell, trains to tolerate seawater, and learns shipbuilding before branching toward a noble shipwright or a cursed Grass/Ghost fate connected to drowning.

#### Carapenter — The Apprentice Shipwright Cosmoid

- **Family:** Approved second evolution of Palmit.
- **Typing:** Grass
- **Name concept:** _Carapace_ + _carpenter_, joining its hardened natural shell to its shipbuilding apprenticeship.
- **Core concept:** An exposed young crab whose own broad carapace has matured into brown, layered, coconut-husk-like armor; it reshapes Palmit's stick into a handmade wooden mallet and practices repairs while training in seawater.
- **Branch role:** Patient craft and care can lead toward the noble shipwright final form, while pushing its incomplete saltwater adaptation to the point of drowning can lead toward the cursed Grass/Ghost branch.
- **Status:** Production-integrated with a game icon, corrected two-claw directional battle art, gameplay data, Palmit's level-16 evolution link, and one capturable Level 5 encounter. Journal art and both final branches remain open.
- **Dedicated record and concept art:** [`docs/cosmoids/carapenter.md`](./docs/cosmoids/carapenter.md)

### Fueguana — The Bandit Cosmoid

- **Core concept:** An island iguana and gunpowder-inspired bandit.
- **Typing:** Fire
- **Entry:** It enjoys causing havoc on pirate ships by devouring gunpowder and setting the decks ablaze. If grabbed by the tail, it detaches the appendage to escape, leaving behind a surprise that explodes moments later!
- **Evolution ideas:** Its impulsive shipboard mischief develops into Velocinder's explosive speed before branching toward either a noble or cursed final evolution.

#### Velocinder — The Fuse-Racing Cosmoid

- **Family:** Approved second evolution of Fueguana.
- **Typing:** Fire
- **Name concept:** _Velocity_ + _cinder_, with a deliberate sound association with _velociraptor_ that reinforces its swift reptilian silhouette.
- **Core concept:** A competitive young fire iguana whose enlarged hind legs and thorned counterbalancing tail let it race lit cannon fuses across pirate decks. Gunpowder-fed heat builds across its whole body and vents as sparks most visibly from its feet and tail.
- **Branch role:** Greater balance and speed can mature into disciplined freedom on the noble route, while cursed influence could twist its competitive drive into frantic, self-destructive motion.
- **Status:** Standalone identity approved and production-integrated with icon, directional battle art, gameplay data, Fueguana evolution logic, and a capturable Level 5 encounter. Journal artwork and both final branches remain open.
- **Dedicated record and concept art:** [`docs/cosmoids/velocinder.md`](./docs/cosmoids/velocinder.md)

### Aguana — The Shore-Grazing Cosmoid

- **Family:** First stage; evolves into Nautiguan.
- **Typing:** Water
- **Name concept:** _Agua_ + _iguana_, directly expressing a young iguana adapted to life at the water's edge.
- **Core concept:** A nimble marine-iguana Cosmoid that makes brief dives for algae and uses oversized hooked claws to cling to slippery rocks against the surf.
- **Entry:** It makes brief dives to graze on algae growing beneath the waves. Its hooked claws grip slippery rocks, keeping it from being swept away before it must return to shore and warm itself.
- **Visual identity:** A long, low coral-and-indigo juvenile with four widely spread clawed feet, an enormous paddle tail, a smooth back, and only a few small salt nubs around the nostrils and front brow.
- **Status:** Standalone identity approved September 21, 2026 and production-integrated September 22, 2026 with an icon, directional battle art, gameplay data, level-16 evolution, capturable Level 6 encounters, and approved journal artwork showing its relaxed belly-up warming behavior.
- **Dedicated record and concept art:** [`docs/cosmoids/aguana.md`](./docs/cosmoids/aguana.md)

#### Nautiguan — The Salt-Crowned Cosmoid

- **Family:** Second and currently final stage of Aguana.
- **Typing:** Water
- **Name concept:** _Nautical_ + _iguana_, connecting its marine-iguana basis to the long voyages on which sailors value it.
- **Core concept:** A broad, heat-retaining marine grazer that dives beyond Aguana's range, propels itself with a massive paddle tail, and forcefully expels salt gathered from seawater.
- **Entry:** Its greater strength and heat-retaining body allow it to graze in deeper water for much longer. It spits excess salt collected from seawater, so sailors often bring one aboard and gather the crystals to replenish the ship's salt reserves.
- **Visual identity:** A squat coral-and-indigo adult with a huge rounded torso, four powerful clawed limbs, a high curved paddle tail, and a dramatic white salt-crystal crown and dorsal fringe.
- **Gameplay direction:** The integrated move set emphasizes water attacks and gripping; reversible size change, a dedicated tail move, and salt-spitting remain future mechanical directions.
- **Status:** Standalone identity approved September 21, 2026 and production-integrated September 22, 2026 with an icon, directional battle art, gameplay data, its role as Aguana's level-16 evolution, and approved underwater journal artwork showing a young Aguana clinging to its sheltered flank in a strong current. Wild placement remains open.
- **Dedicated record and concept art:** [`docs/cosmoids/nautiguan.md`](./docs/cosmoids/nautiguan.md)

### Monkeg — The Stowaway Cosmoid

- **Core concept:** A combination of sea monkeys, monkeys in a barrel, and a macaque.
- **Typing:** Water
- **Entry:** A shy Cosmoid that hides inside barrels to get from place to place unseen. It uses its tail to navigate the oceans.
- **Evolution ideas:** It leaves its barrel, repurposes it as a weighted utility pack, and earns strength and practical wisdom through deck work before branching into a noble or cursed final evolution.

#### Macask — The Deckhand Cosmoid

- **Family:** Approved second evolution of Monkeg.
- **Typing:** Water
- **Name concept:** _Macaque_ + _cask_, joining its coastal-primate basis to the barrel it carries for the crew.
- **Core concept:** A former stowaway that wears its old barrel as a cargo-filled backpack, turning its hiding place into a chosen burden that builds strength, balance, and responsibility.
- **Branch role:** Honest service and shared responsibility can lead to Astrolape, while exploitative overloading with cursed salvage can twist its persistence toward the cursed final branch.
- **Status:** Standalone identity approved and production-integrated with icon, directional battle art, gameplay data, Monkeg evolution logic, and a capturable Level 5 encounter. Journal art and the final branches remain open.
- **Dedicated record and concept art:** [`docs/cosmoids/macask.md`](./docs/cosmoids/macask.md)

#### Astrolape — The Navigator Cosmoid

- **Family:** Proposed third evolution of the Monkeg family.
- **Concept details:**
  - Holds and attacks with a barrel full of water, similar to Donkey Kong.
  - Has a seaweed cape and wooden shoulder pad that make it look like a captain.
  - Has long white hair and a Cosmoid tail.

### Senticle — The False Eel Cosmoid

- **Core concept:** It looks like an eel, but is actually a single severed tentacle from a budding Kraken that gained sentience. It also draws inspiration from a lamprey eel.
- **Typing:** Water
- **Entry:** Pirate grunts often fish these up thinking they are eels. It is actually a small part of a much larger creature waiting to regrow its body.

### Hookaw — The Hook Cosmoid

- **Core concept:** A Puerto Rican parrot with a steel, hook-shaped beak. It gets into fights and can lose a leg.
- **Typing:** Flying/Steel
- **Entry:** Their beaks and wings are made of sharp steel. This one seems to have lost a leg while battling another Hookaw.

## Sailkworm Evolution Family

### Sailkworm — The Worm Cosmoid

- **Stage:** 1 (grub)
- **Core concept:** A grub that loves to eat ship sails, much to sailors' annoyance.
- **Typing:** Bug
- **Entry:** It loves to eat ship sails, much to sailors' annoyance. Its body resembles different pieces of stitched cloth, helping it go undetected aboard ships.

### Cocoonot — The Knot Cosmoid

- **Stage:** 2 (pupa)
- **Core concept:** A cocoon resembling a tangled rat's nest of rope and knots tied tightly to a ship's rigging.
- **Typing:** Bug
- **Entry:** Made of a strong string resembling coconut husk, it withstands hurricane winds by tying itself securely to the mast.
- **Additional lore:** Often mistaken for a stash of coconuts in a ship's rigging, this pupa is actually a nearly indestructible ball of knots. Sailors believe that anyone who unties a Cocoonot without cutting the rope will be blessed with fair winds forever, but its knots are pulled so tightly that doing so is physically impossible.

### Mainscale — The Moth Cosmoid

- **Stage:** 3 (moth)
- **Core concept:** A moth with massive, stiff wings that catch the wind like sails. It generates gusts to help ships move.
- **Typing:** Bug
- **Entry:** Its massive sail-like wings can generate enough wind to help a ship move.

## Shared Starter Evolution Ideas

- All starters could have branched third-stage evolutions representing an **Honor** path or a **Lawless** path.
- One possible mechanic gives the second stage a rage or evil aura. A quest offers the player the chance to remove it; that choice determines the final branch.
- Even with a shared aura mechanic, each creature would evolve through a different method depending on whether it still has the aura.
- Evolution and discovery clues could come from dialogue, journal notes, or Cosmoid entries. For example, an NPC might mention that the Monkeg family likes to view the stars from as close as possible. This hints that taking its second stage to a high point without the evil aura will trigger its honorable evolution.

### Alligator Gar Directions

Several alternate concepts and typings are being considered for an alligator gar Cosmoid.

#### Voltgar

- **Typing:** Water/Electric
- **Core concept:** An alligator gar whose long snout functions as an electrical stun gun.
- **Entry idea:** Its elongated snout acts as a bioelectrical node. By swimming rapidly, it generates enough static charge to stun an entire pond of fish in an instant.

#### Garmour

- **Typing:** Water/Steel
- **Core concept:** An alligator gar with natural knightly plate armor and a jousting lance.
- **Entry idea:** It patrols murky swamps like a noble knight. Its scales are harder than modern steel, effortlessly deflecting blows before it retaliates with its lance-like snout.

#### Gargoylar

- **Typing:** Water/Rock
- **Core concept:** A prehistoric alligator gar whose tough scales have fully calcified into rock.
- **Entry idea:** Restored from an ancient fossil, its scales have fossilized into impenetrable stone. It uses its heavy, stone-crushing jaws to bite through solid rock.

### Lionfish Directions

#### Scorpicac

- **Theme:** Arid terrestrial adaptation — an aquatic creature that adapted to thrive in bone-dry desert sand as though it were water.
- **Typing:** Poison/Grass
- **Core concept:** A terrestrial lionfish that swims through dunes, with fins adapted into venomous cactus pads.
- **Entry idea:** Having lost its ocean habitat millennia ago, it learned to swim through scorching desert sands. The venom in its spines is now mixed with a potent hallucinogenic sap that deters desert scavengers.

#### Submarox

- **Theme:** Steampunk oceanic engineering — an organic creature that naturally grows mechanical-looking parts to survive extreme pressure.
- **Typing:** Poison/Steel
- **Core concept:** A heavy armored lionfish resembling an early toxic-steam-powered submarine.
- **Entry idea:** After adapting to the crushing pressure of the abyssal zone, its skin hardened into heavy metal plating. It moves by expelling pressurized toxic steam from its brass-like spines, sounding like an old submarine.
