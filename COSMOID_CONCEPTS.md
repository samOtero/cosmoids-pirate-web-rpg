# Cosmoid Concept Catalog

This document is the working reference for Cosmoid creature concepts currently under consideration. Details are exploratory unless they have already been implemented in the game; names, typings, lore, and evolution paths may change.

Last consolidated: August 30, 2026

## Developed Concepts

### Palmit — The Castaway Cosmoid

- **Core concept:** A freshwater crab that uses a coconut as a boat to protect itself from salt water while traveling across the ocean.
- **Typing:** Grass
- **Entry:** A freshwater crab that uses a coconut as a boat to protect itself from salt water when traveling the ocean.
- **Evolution ideas:** It grows able to handle salt water and becomes a better sailor. Its story starts happily, but by the third evolution it meets its end and becomes a ghost pirate.

### Fueguana — The Bandit Cosmoid

- **Core concept:** An island iguana and gunpowder-inspired bandit.
- **Typing:** Fire
- **Entry:** It enjoys causing havoc on pirate ships by devouring gunpowder and setting the decks ablaze. If grabbed by the tail, it detaches the appendage to escape, leaving behind a surprise that explodes moments later!
- **Evolution ideas:** The sack around its neck could become a bandana. It starts as a bandit and ultimately becomes a noble swordsman.

### Monkeg — The Stowaway Cosmoid

- **Core concept:** A combination of sea monkeys, monkeys in a barrel, and a macaque.
- **Typing:** Water
- **Entry:** A shy Cosmoid that hides inside barrels to get from place to place unseen. It uses its tail to navigate the oceans.
- **Evolution ideas:** Donkey Kong inspiration.

#### Astrolape — The Navigator Cosmoid

- **Family:** Proposed third evolution of the Monkeg family.
- **Concept details:**
  - Holds and attacks with a barrel full of water, similar to Donkey Kong.
  - Has a seaweed cape and wooden shoulder pad that make it look like a captain.
  - Has long white hair and a Cosmoid tail.

### Senticle — The False Eel Cosmoid

- **Core concept:** It looks like an eel, but is actually a single severed tentacle from a budding Kraken that gained sentience. It also draws inspiration from a lamprey eel.
- **Typing:** Water
- **Entry:** Pirate grunts often fish these up thinking they are eels. It is actually a small part of a much larger creature waiting to regrow its body.

### Hookaw — The Hook Cosmoid

- **Core concept:** A Puerto Rican parrot with a steel, hook-shaped beak. It gets into fights and can lose a leg.
- **Typing:** Flying/Steel
- **Entry:** Their beaks and wings are made of sharp steel. This one seems to have lost a leg while battling another Hookaw.

## Sailkworm Evolution Family

### Sailkworm — The Worm Cosmoid

- **Stage:** 1 (grub)
- **Core concept:** A grub that loves to eat ship sails, much to sailors' annoyance.
- **Typing:** Bug
- **Entry:** It loves to eat ship sails, much to sailors' annoyance. Its body resembles different pieces of stitched cloth, helping it go undetected aboard ships.

### Cocoonot — The Knot Cosmoid

- **Stage:** 2 (pupa)
- **Core concept:** A cocoon resembling a tangled rat's nest of rope and knots tied tightly to a ship's rigging.
- **Typing:** Bug
- **Entry:** Made of a strong string resembling coconut husk, it withstands hurricane winds by tying itself securely to the mast.
- **Additional lore:** Often mistaken for a stash of coconuts in a ship's rigging, this pupa is actually a nearly indestructible ball of knots. Sailors believe that anyone who unties a Cocoonot without cutting the rope will be blessed with fair winds forever, but its knots are pulled so tightly that doing so is physically impossible.

### Mainscale — The Moth Cosmoid

- **Stage:** 3 (moth)
- **Core concept:** A moth with massive, stiff wings that catch the wind like sails. It generates gusts to help ships move.
- **Typing:** Bug
- **Entry:** Its massive sail-like wings can generate enough wind to help a ship move.

## Shared Starter Evolution Ideas

- All starters could have branched third-stage evolutions representing an **Honor** path or a **Lawless** path.
- One possible mechanic gives the second stage a rage or evil aura. A quest offers the player the chance to remove it; that choice determines the final branch.
- Even with a shared aura mechanic, each creature would evolve through a different method depending on whether it still has the aura.
- Evolution and discovery clues could come from dialogue, journal notes, or Cosmoid entries. For example, an NPC might mention that the Monkeg family likes to view the stars from as close as possible. This hints that taking its second stage to a high point without the evil aura will trigger its honorable evolution.

## Concept Backlog

These are earlier-stage seeds and alternate directions that have not yet been consolidated into complete families.

### Corrogoon — The Corrosive Excavator

- **Typing:** Poison/Ground
- **Core concept:** A vinegaroon-inspired prisoner whose acidic secretion carries corrosion-promoting microbes. It saturates loose grit with the secretion and lashes the abrasive slurry from its fan-shaped tail to help break Tigrevel's steel gate in Level 5.
- **Name concept:** Corrosion or corrode + vinegaroon, connecting its animal basis to its metal-scouring behavior.
- **Status:** Creature assets, Poison/Ground typing, registry entry, and manifest implemented; Level 5 captive-ally behavior remains planned.
- **Dedicated record and concept art:** [`docs/cosmoids/corrogoon.md`](./docs/cosmoids/corrogoon.md)

### Bramfrit — The Courtly Battering Ram

- **Typing:** Fire
- **Core concept:** A proud bighorn-ram prisoner whose squared spiral horns align into one heated battering face. It bows, retreats exactly three paces, scratches a glowing starting line, and waits for a gate to accept its formal challenge before charging.
- **Name concept:** Battering ram + ifrit, expressing a siege creature animated by a fiery spirit.
- **Status:** Creature assets, registry entry, and manifest implemented; Level 5 captive-ally behavior remains planned.
- **Dedicated record and concept art:** [`docs/cosmoids/bramfrit.md`](./docs/cosmoids/bramfrit.md)

### Sepippet — The Shadow-Stage Infiltrator

- **Typing:** Dark
- **Core concept:** A theatrical cuttlefish prisoner whose chromatophore mantle projects false Navy guards while its broad arms flatten into articulated shadows, slip through the gate's seams, and remove its internal locking pieces.
- **Character hook:** An accomplished illusionist that desperately wants to appear mysterious, but one excitable arm always waves at the audience and reveals the trick.
- **Name concept:** _Sepia_ + _puppet_, connecting its cuttlefish biology to its shadow-puppet performances.
- **Status:** Creature assets, registry entry, Dark typing, and manifest implemented; Level 5 captive-ally behavior remains planned.
- **Dedicated record and concept art:** [`docs/cosmoids/sepippet.md`](./docs/cosmoids/sepippet.md)

### Parlet — The Restless Reef Artist

- **Typing:** Water
- **Core concept:** A proud parrotfish whose broad overlapping scales rearrange their colors into living mosaic patterns. Every finished design lasts only until Parlet notices one imperfect patch and starts revising it again.
- **Character hook:** A gifted artist that is physically incapable of deciding its own work is finished.
- **Name concept:** _Parrotfish_ + _palette_, connecting its animal basis with the living colors across its body.
- **Status:** Creature assets, registry entry, Water typing, and manifest implemented; Level 5 captive-ally behavior remains planned.
- **Dedicated record and concept art:** [`docs/cosmoids/parlet.md`](./docs/cosmoids/parlet.md)

### Alligator Gar Directions

Several alternate concepts and typings are being considered for an alligator gar Cosmoid.

#### Voltgar

- **Typing:** Water/Electric
- **Core concept:** An alligator gar whose long snout functions as an electrical stun gun.
- **Entry idea:** Its elongated snout acts as a bioelectrical node. By swimming rapidly, it generates enough static charge to stun an entire pond of fish in an instant.

#### Garmour

- **Typing:** Water/Steel
- **Core concept:** An alligator gar with natural knightly plate armor and a jousting lance.
- **Entry idea:** It patrols murky swamps like a noble knight. Its scales are harder than modern steel, effortlessly deflecting blows before it retaliates with its lance-like snout.

#### Gargoylar

- **Typing:** Water/Rock
- **Core concept:** A prehistoric alligator gar whose tough scales have fully calcified into rock.
- **Entry idea:** Restored from an ancient fossil, its scales have fossilized into impenetrable stone. It uses its heavy, stone-crushing jaws to bite through solid rock.

### Lionfish Directions

#### Scorpicac

- **Theme:** Arid terrestrial adaptation — an aquatic creature that adapted to thrive in bone-dry desert sand as though it were water.
- **Typing:** Poison/Grass
- **Core concept:** A terrestrial lionfish that swims through dunes, with fins adapted into venomous cactus pads.
- **Entry idea:** Having lost its ocean habitat millennia ago, it learned to swim through scorching desert sands. The venom in its spines is now mixed with a potent hallucinogenic sap that deters desert scavengers.

#### Submarox

- **Theme:** Steampunk oceanic engineering — an organic creature that naturally grows mechanical-looking parts to survive extreme pressure.
- **Typing:** Poison/Steel
- **Core concept:** A heavy armored lionfish resembling an early toxic-steam-powered submarine.
- **Entry idea:** After adapting to the crushing pressure of the abyssal zone, its skin hardened into heavy metal plating. It moves by expelling pressurized toxic steam from its brass-like spines, sounding like an old submarine.

### Boat Dog Directions

#### Mopup

- **Typing:** Normal
- **Core concept:** A helpful pup whose fur naturally absorbs seawater and cleans the ship deck as it plays.
- **Entry idea:** Extremely playful, it loves sliding across wet surfaces. The more it plays, the cleaner the ship deck becomes thanks to its highly absorbent coat.
- **Character note:** This might suit a Kody-like character: it starts young and dedicated to cleaning, then evolves into a strong fighter or captain.

### Stone Dog Directions

#### Lighthound

- **Typing:** Rock/Electric
- **Core concept:** A petrified dog that became a living lighthouse beacon to guide its owner home.
- **Entry idea:** It refused to leave the dock during a massive storm. A lightning strike crystallized its loyalty, turning it into a living stone beacon for lost sailors.

#### Sandruff

- **Typing:** Ground/Rock
- **Core concept:** A coastal dog whose endless waiting merged it with the sand of the beach.
- **Entry idea:** It sat on the beach for so long that it became one with the dunes. Its barks are said to sound like the howling sea wind.

### Zapybara

- **Typing:** Electric
- **Core concept:** An electric capybara that uses an immense static charge to float like a storm cloud.
- **Entry idea:** Its fur is so dry and dense that simply moving causes an extreme static buildup. It stores enough electricity to constantly repel the ground, allowing it to float through the air.
- **Behavior note:** They travel in packs of hundreds, resembling clouds in the sky and creating unnatural thunderstorms when annoyed.

### Petroqui

- **Entry idea:** Said to have been carved by ancient island dwellers, this stone Cosmoid awakens at night to sing melodies that soothe troubled minds.

### Caffiqui

- **Typing:** Grass/Fairy
- **Core concept:** A coffee bean that produces an energizing sound.
- **Entry idea:** Hiding among coffee plantations, this tiny Cosmoid sings a high-pitched melody at dawn. Anyone who hears its song feels instantly awake and energized.

### Pelicargo

- **Theme:** Living freight.
- **Typing:** Normal/Flying
- **Core concept:** A pelican that operates as an aerial delivery system.
- **Entry idea:** It transports items and small Cosmoids across vast distances in its elastic throat pouch. It never drops a single item during flight.

### Sculptacean

- **Typing:** Ice
- **Core concept:** An artisan pistol shrimp that uses sonic snaps to chisel ice into different shapes.
- **Entry idea:** Using high-frequency snaps from its claw, it carves massive blocks of ice into intricate shapes. It creates substitutes of itself to help it escape predators.

### Manatee Directions

- **Typing:** Fairy
- **Core concept:** A pillow-like manatee that serves as the ultimate cuddly sleep aid.

#### Manatree

### Knotia

- **Inspiration:** Mouse or hutia.
- **Typing:** Normal
- **Core concept:** A port-dwelling rodent whose gnawing habits inadvertently create perfect maritime knots.
- **Entry idea:** While chewing on mooring lines at the docks, it accidentally creates incredibly complex knots that even seasoned sailors struggle to untie.

### Harlequill

- **Inspiration:** Laughing gull or another coastal bird.
- **Typing:** Flying
- **Core concept:** A jester-themed gull that brings happiness to coastal towns with its aerial comedy routines.

### Snoutasco

- **Inspiration:** Solenodon, with some Nidoran influence.
- **Typing:** Fire/Poison
- **Core concept:** A spicy solenodon whose venom is effectively weapons-grade hot sauce.
