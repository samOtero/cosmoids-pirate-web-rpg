# Level 8 — Harbor Tournament Prototype

> First playable placeholder added October 2, 2026. The approved story direction lives in [the tournament arc outline](harbor-tournament-arc.md). This entry is a layout and combat sandbox; it does not play the quarterfinal story or finalize the storyteller's creature roster.

## Current Slice

- Available after completing Shore Leave, as **Harbor Tournament — Prototype**.
- One wide vector arena with both flag locations, opposing routes, player placement spots, and rival-side placement guides visible together.
- The camera contains the entire arena within the available viewport, including on narrow screens, without requiring panning.
- Incoming runners attack the blue flag, while automatic allied runners with blue ground markers travel up the upper route and strike the red flag. Both flags have visible life counters.
- A stationary rival defender fires practice shots at allied runners. Player towers can defeat it to open the offensive route; it does not recover yet.
- Reduce the rival flag's lives to zero before losing your own flag. Clearing incoming waves alone does not end this duel. Allied runners are temporary autonomous units and do not join the roster or earn persistent experience.
- Knotia and Harlequill are temporary test stand-ins. They are not the storyteller's canonical partners; the tournament characters will introduce new Cosmoid species.
- Capture is disabled. Completing practice returns to level select without awarding a new story milestone or unlocking a placeholder-species journal entry.
- The registered level can be opened and saved through the existing level editor and shared source revision protocol.

The arena has been widened to approximately 2.6:1, while preserving the single-screen camera. Runners now contest both flags. The rival's practice shots target allied runners; attacks against player towers, recovery, and mirrored defender rules remain future prototype work.

## Next Prototype Work

- Damage-dealing, telegraphed ranged attacks against player defenders.
- Knockout recovery, redeployment, and opponent recovery under comparable rules.
- Compare offensive orders from defensive positions with contested central placements.
- Replace the temporary species and vector arena after their designs are approved.
- Draft the arrival, entry, quarterfinal, and aftermath dialogue within the story panel limits.

Gameplay tuning remains in the authored level and combat registries; this document does not duplicate a balance specification.
